Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

ExamBook Pro respects your privacy. This policy explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you use this website.

If you create an account or use account-related features, information needed for authentication and your profile may be processed by the services used by the website. ExamBook Pro uses Supabase services for authentication and related application data.

Test and performance data

When account-based test features are used, information such as attempted tests, scores and related performance history may be stored so that the website can provide profile and progress features.

The website or third-party services used by it may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for authentication, security, functionality or advertising.

Advertising

ExamBook Pro may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense after the website is approved and configured for advertising. Such services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalise advertising in accordance with their own policies.

Third-party services

The website may depend on third-party services, including Supabase and content delivery services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Children

ExamBook Pro is an educational resource. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information unnecessarily may contact us through the Contact Us page.

Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used by the services supporting the website, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please visit our Contact Us page.
