About ExamBook Pro

ExamBook Pro is an online learning and mock-test platform designed to help students and competitive-exam aspirants practise in a simple, accessible format.

What we provide

  • Competitive exam mock tests
  • English and Hindi practice content
  • Railway, SSC, Bihar, BPSC and CTET preparation resources
  • Class 7–10 Science practice tests
  • Study and revision resources

Our goal

Our goal is to make regular practice easier by providing structured tests, exam-style timing, question palettes and performance-oriented practice.

Independent educational resource

ExamBook Pro is an independent educational website. Unless specifically stated, we are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any examination authority, government department, board, or recruiting organisation.
